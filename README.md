## .com, .in, .co.in or .net?

| | .com | .in | .co.in | .net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First year price | $3.30 | $1.09 | $1.09 | $4.41 |
| Best for | Global brands | Indian websites | Indian commerce | Tech and network brands |
| India focus | Neutral | Strong | Strong | Neutral |
| Global reach | Excellent | Moderate | Low | Good |
| WHOIS privacy | Free | Free | Free | Not stated on this page |

How to choose:

- **Choose .com** if you want to serve customers in India and abroad, or you plan to grow beyond India.
- **Choose .in** if your customers are mostly in India. It is India's country code domain, and it signals a local business.
- **Choose .co.in** if you are an Indian commercial business and want that style.
- **Choose .net** if you run a technology or network focused brand.
- **Register two** if the name matters to you. Many businesses take the .com and the .in together, so nobody else can use their brand name.

If you are only targeting India, a .in may give you a small local search advantage, but the extension is not a ranking shortcut. Good content and a fast site matter far more.

### The DNS Records You Will Use Most

You manage these from your YouStable dashboard.

| Record | What It Does |
|---|---|
